# Transcription and Data Cleaning Sample

## Project Overview

This sample demonstrates principles used in transcription and basic data cleaning.

## Transcription Approach

* Preserve the meaning of the original audio.
* Remove unnecessary filler words when clean verbatim is required.
* Correct obvious grammar and punctuation issues.
* Clearly identify different speakers.
* Maintain consistent formatting.
* Review the transcript for accuracy before submission.

## Sample

**Speaker 1:** Good morning. Could you please confirm the meeting time?

**Speaker 2:** Yes. The meeting is scheduled for 10:00 AM.

## Data Quality Checks

Before submitting a completed transcript, I would check for:

1. Missing words or sentences.
2. Incorrect speaker labels.
3. Spelling and punctuation errors.
4. Inconsistent formatting.
5. Unclear or incorrectly transcribed information.
